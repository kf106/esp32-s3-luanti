Doors and trapdoors: models, textures and sounds from Minetest Game's doors mod
(https://github.com/luanti-org/minetest_game, mods/doors), unchanged:

  door_a.b3d, door_b.b3d
  doors_door_wood.png, doors_door_steel.png, doors_door_glass.png
  doors_item_wood.png, doors_item_steel.png, doors_item_glass.png
  doors_door_open.ogg, doors_door_close.ogg, doors_steel_door_open.ogg,
  doors_steel_door_close.ogg, doors_glass_door_open.ogg, doors_glass_door_close.ogg
  doors_trapdoor.png, doors_trapdoor_side.png, doors_trapdoor_steel.png,
  doors_trapdoor_steel_side.png
