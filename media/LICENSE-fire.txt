Fire: textures and sounds from Minetest Game's fire mod
(https://github.com/luanti-org/minetest_game, mods/fire), under their
original names. The fire_fire.*.ogg sounds were re-encoded at Vorbis
quality 3 (from about 110 kbit/s to 80) to save flash; the rest are
unchanged:

  fire_basic_flame.png, fire_basic_flame_animated.png, fire_flint_steel.png,
  fire_fire.1.ogg, fire_fire.2.ogg, fire_fire.3.ogg,
  fire_extinguish_flame.1.ogg, fire_extinguish_flame.2.ogg,
  fire_extinguish_flame.3.ogg, fire_flint_and_steel.ogg
