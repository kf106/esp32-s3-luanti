Flowers and dyes: textures from Minetest Game's flowers and dye mods
(https://github.com/luanti-org/minetest_game, mods/flowers and mods/dye), unchanged:

  flowers_rose.png, flowers_tulip.png, flowers_dandelion_yellow.png,
  flowers_chrysanthemum_green.png, flowers_geranium.png, flowers_viola.png,
  flowers_dandelion_white.png, flowers_tulip_black.png, flowers_mushroom_red.png,
  flowers_mushroom_brown.png, flowers_waterlily.png, flowers_waterlily_bottom.png
  dye_*.png (15 colours)
